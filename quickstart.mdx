---
title: "Get started with VadedTV"
description: "Learn how to create a VadedTV account, explore the home feed, save titles to My List, and start streaming from multiple sources in four steps."
---

VadedTV lets you discover, track, and watch movies and TV series from a single interface. This guide walks you through creating an account, exploring the home feed, saving titles to your list, and opening a streaming source — all in four steps.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Go to VadedTV and choose how you want to sign in:

    - **Email and password** — enter your email address and create a password.
    - **Google** — click "Continue with Google" to sign in with your existing Google account via Google OAuth.
    - **Anonymous** — start browsing immediately without an account. You can upgrade to a full account later to save your watchlist across devices.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Browse the home feed">
    After signing in you land on the home feed. It is organized into several sections:

    - **Trending** — movies and TV series that are popular right now.
    - **Genres** — filter by action, comedy, drama, thriller, and more.
    - **Recommendations** — titles suggested based on what you have added to your list.

    Scroll horizontally through any row to see more titles. Click a poster to open the detail panel for that title.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add titles to My List">
    On any title's detail panel, click the **+ My List** button to save it for later. Your list is accessible from the navigation bar at any time.

    To remove a title, open it again and click the same button — it will now show as already added so you can remove it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open a title and select a streaming source">
    From a title's detail panel, click **Watch Now**. VadedTV aggregates multiple streaming sources for each title. A source selector appears at the top of the player — pick any source to start playback. If one source is unavailable, switch to another with a single click.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  VadedTV is a Progressive Web App (PWA). On supported browsers you will see an install prompt. Installing the PWA gives you a home screen shortcut, offline access, and quick-launch shortcuts for **Movies** and **TV Shows** directly from your device.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Use the search bar at the top of any page to find a specific title by name. Results include both movies and TV series ranked by relevance. You can also share a title link — VadedTV generates rich Open Graph previews so the title's poster and description appear automatically when you paste the link into Discord, Telegram, WhatsApp, or other messaging apps.
</Tip>
