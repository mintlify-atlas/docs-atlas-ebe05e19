---
title: "Anonymous username accounts on VadedTV"
description: "Create a VadedTV account with just a username and password — no email required. Optionally link an email later to secure your account."
---

Username accounts let you join VadedTV without providing an email address. All you need is a username and a password. You get the same access as any other account — My List, watch history, and chat — and you can optionally add an email later if you want password recovery.

## Creating a username account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the sign-up page">
    Navigate to the VadedTV sign-up page. You will see two tabs at the top of the form: **Use Email** and **Choose Username**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select &quot;Choose Username&quot;">
    Click the **Choose Username** tab to switch to the username account form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a username">
    Type the display name you want to use. Usernames must be between 2 and 20 characters and can only contain letters, numbers, and underscores — for example `Shadow`, `shadow_99`, or `CoolUser`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a password">
    Create a password for your account. Passwords must be at least 6 characters long.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click &quot;Create Username Account&quot;">
    Submit the form. VadedTV creates your account and assigns you a unique discriminator tag.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your tag">
    After your account is created, your full tag — for example `Shadow#4821` — is displayed on screen. Save this tag somewhere safe. You will need it if another user shares your display name.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Understanding your tag

Your display name is not required to be unique. Multiple users can choose the name `Shadow`. To tell accounts apart, VadedTV automatically assigns each one a four-digit discriminator, forming a unique tag like `Shadow#4821`.

The tag is the combination of your chosen name and your discriminator, separated by `#`. Two users can both be `Shadow`, but `Shadow#4821` and `Shadow#3302` are always distinct accounts.

<Note>
Your discriminator is assigned automatically when your account is created. You cannot choose it yourself.
</Note>

## Signing in to your username account

On the VadedTV login page, select the **Username** tab. You can sign in using either format:

- Just your display name — `Shadow`
- Your full tag — `Shadow#4821`

If you type only your display name and more than one account shares it, VadedTV will ask you to enter your full tag to identify which account you mean.

<Tip>
Using your full tag when signing in (e.g. `Shadow#4821`) avoids any ambiguity and is faster if you share a name with another user.
</Tip>

## Linking an email to your account

You can add an email address to your username account at any time from Settings. This enables password recovery without changing anything else about your account.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Navigate to your account Settings from the navigation menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the Link Email option">
    In your profile section, click the **Link Email** button to open the email linking dialog.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your email address">
    Type the email address you want to link to your account and click **Link Email**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once your email is linked, your account gains password recovery capability. Your username and discriminator tag remain unchanged.

## What username accounts can do

Username accounts have access to the same features as email and Google accounts:

- **My List** — save titles to watch later, synced to your account
- **Watch history** — your recently watched titles are tracked and synced
- **Chat** — participate in community features

## If you forget your password

<Warning>
If you forget your password and have not linked an email address to your account, there is no way to recover access. There is no email recovery path without a linked email.
</Warning>

To protect against this, link an email address in Settings as soon as possible after creating your account. It only takes a moment and ensures you are never permanently locked out.
