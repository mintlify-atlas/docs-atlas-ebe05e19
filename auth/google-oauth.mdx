---
title: "Sign in with Google on VadedTV"
description: "Use your Google account to sign in to VadedTV instantly. No password needed — your profile picture and name are imported automatically."
---

You can sign in to VadedTV using your existing Google account. There is no separate password to create or remember — Google handles the authentication and VadedTV receives your name and profile picture automatically.

## How to sign in with Google

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the login or sign-up page">
    Go to the VadedTV login page or sign-up page. Both pages include the Google sign-in option.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click &quot;Continue with Google&quot;">
    Below the sign-in form, click the **Continue with Google** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your Google account">
    A Google account selector will open in your browser. Choose the account you want to use. If you are not already signed in to Google, you will be prompted to sign in first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You're redirected back to VadedTV">
    After you confirm with Google, you are automatically returned to VadedTV and signed in. The whole process takes just a few seconds.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What gets imported from Google

When you first sign in with Google, VadedTV creates a VadedTV account using:

- **Your name** — as it appears on your Google account
- **Your profile picture** — pulled directly from your Google account avatar

<Note>
If this is your first time signing in with Google, your VadedTV account is created automatically. You do not need to go through a separate sign-up step.
</Note>

## Signing in on production

On the production version of VadedTV, after Google confirms your identity you are redirected back to VadedTV to complete the sign-in. The redirect happens automatically and you should be signed in within a moment of returning to the site.

<Tip>
If you are ever asked to sign in again after being redirected back to VadedTV, try refreshing the page. The sign-in process may still be completing in the background.
</Tip>

## Switching accounts

If you want to use a different Google account, sign out of VadedTV first, then click **Continue with Google** again. Google will give you the option to select a different account.
