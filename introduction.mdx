## What is VadedTV?

VadedTV is a full-stack streaming discovery platform powered by [TMDB](https://www.themoviedb.org/). It combines a rich browsing experience with personal tracking tools and real-time community features.

## Get started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an account">
    Sign up at VadedTV with your email address, continue with Google, or create an anonymous username account — no email required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse and discover">
    Explore the home feed for trending movies and series. Use filters to narrow by genre, rating, year, and language.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build your watchlist">
    Add titles to **My List** from any movie card or detail modal. Your list syncs automatically across sessions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch and chat">
    Open any title and choose a streaming source. Join the live community chat to discuss with other viewers in real time.
  </Step>
</Steps>
